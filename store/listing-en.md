## Extension name

Immotop Companion

## Short description (132 characters max)

Block a seller or agency on immotop.lu search results in one click.

## Detailed description

**Immotop Companion** adds a filter immotop.lu doesn't have: block, in one click, every listing
from a given professional seller or agency on search results — handy for agencies that keep
reposting the same listing, list at unrealistic prices, or that you simply don't want to see
anymore.

**How it works**

On an immotop.lu search results page, a small button appears when you hover an agency's logo.
One click adds the seller to your block list and blurs their listings on the page right away,
with a clear message and a "Show anyway" button to check one occasionally without unblocking it.

**Key features**

- Block a seller in one click, right from the search results
- Listings are blurred, not removed — a clear message, nothing destructive, fully reversible
- Sellers are matched by their logo image, not their name, avoiding mix-ups between agencies
  with similar names
- Also works on listings loaded dynamically (infinite scroll, pagination)
- Management panel: view blocked sellers, remove one, export/import the list as JSON (handy for
  backups or syncing across browsers)
- Available in French, German, English, Portuguese, and Luxembourgish, with automatic browser
  language detection

**Privacy**

Immotop Companion does not collect, transmit, or sell any data. Everything is stored locally in
your browser (`chrome.storage.local`): your blocked-seller list and language preference. Nothing
is sent to any external server. Source code is public and open to audit:
https://github.com/clawfire/immotop-companion

**Note**

Independent project, not affiliated with immotop.lu.
